## The A1 stop: T1 speed limit

`J --axis 1 --deg 30 --speed 30 --log runs/s4_a1_v30_b.csv` stopped again, twice, with
`LinkLost(500ms)` on the PC. The pendant showed two messages with the same time stamp:

- `KSS03015 Stop because $SPEED_LIMIT_TEACH_MODE has been exceeded` (originator KS)
- `KSS00489 Stop by $CORRECTION function (reset or block selection required)` (originator A2)

So the controller stopped the robot because the Cartesian speed in T1 went over its teach-mode
limit (250 mm/s); KSS00489 is the consequence. The PC side was clean in every run (`Delay` 0).

| Run | Start A1 | Stopped after | A1 speed, commanded / actual | Tool point radius from A1 | Tool point speed, commanded / actual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-05 `s4_a1_v30.csv` | -77.5° | 0.78 s | 28 / 27 °/s | 333 mm | 163 / 158 mm/s |
| 2026-11-11 `s4_a1_v30_b.csv` (first) | -98.9° | 0.43 s | 15 / 13 °/s | 938 mm | 236 to 245 / 210 to 220 mm/s |
| 2026-11-11 `s4_a1_v30_b.csv` (second, overwrote the first) | -96.5° | 0.43 s | 14 to 15 / 13 °/s | 938 mm | 232 to 245 / 208 mm/s |

Speeds are from the last 8 cycles of each log (the upper value of a range is the last cycle);
the tool point is the `RIst` position in the telegrams. With the arm stretched out the stop
comes as the commanded tool speed reaches about 245 mm/s. The session 2 stop at 163 mm/s was
not read on the pendant; if it was the same message, the controller monitors a point further
from the A1 axis than the reported tool point in that pose (open).

Consequence: in T1 the usable joint speed depends on the pose. Around A1 it is roughly
`250 mm/s / radius`: 15 °/s at 938 mm. Faster moves need T2 or automatic mode.
